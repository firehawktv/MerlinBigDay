About October Big Day

Twice a year, people all over the world count birds on the same day and share what they find with scientists. This page is an independent classroom tool and is not affiliated with Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Want to join the global count too? Make a free account at eBird, or try the free Merlin Bird ID app. You don't need either to take part in our class count.
